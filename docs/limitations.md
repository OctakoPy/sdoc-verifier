# Limitations

- The parser vocabulary is intentionally small. Unseen labels, unusual layouts, handwriting, rotated scans, and complex tables can require manual review.
- The seven-field comparison is a documentation check, not a complete transportation, customs, sanctions, or legal validation system.
- Normalization handles case, whitespace, separators, numeric units, container forms, and one port-code equivalence pattern. It does not infer semantic truth or resolve ambiguous names.
- The local demo has no authentication because it is intended for loopback development. Do not expose it directly to a network.
- Gemini OCR and label translation are optional and experimental. Model output can vary, and the service can send document content to a third party when explicitly enabled.
- The fictional examples are synthetic and in-distribution. They cannot support claims about independent real-world performance.
- There is no durable operator workflow, document upload, mail delivery, database, or hosted service in this portfolio version.
- A result is evidence for review, not an authorization to release cargo or amend a title document.
